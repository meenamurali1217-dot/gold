Gold Investment & Savings Schemes

In all our structured accumulation schemes, you pay for your scheduled monthly installments, and Shalom Gold funds the bonus months directly into your account balance. For example:
  • 1 Year Plan: Pay 11 months · Shalom Gold adds 1 month free (12th month).
  • 2 Years Plan: Pay 22 months · Shalom Gold adds 2 months free.
  • 3 Years Plan: Pay 33 months · Shalom Gold adds 3 months free.
  • 4 Years Plan: Pay 44 months · Shalom Gold adds 4 months free.
  • 5 Years Plan: Pay 55 months · Shalom Gold adds 5 months free.

The minimum installment starts at just ₹1,000 per month for the 1 Year Smart Gold Plan (in multiples of ₹500), ₹2,000 for the 2 Years Wealth Plan, ₹3,000 for the 3 Years Sovereign Plan, and ₹5,000 for the 4 and 5 Years Plans.

Upon maturity, 100% of your accumulated value (your contributions + Shalom Gold bonus contributions) is redeemable in physical gold format: 24K (999) certified gold coins, minted bullion bars, or bespoke 22K (916) hallmarked jewellery at any official Shalom Gold boutique nationwide, or delivered via insured armed transit.

Yes! Scheme members receive exclusive VIP making charges discount waivers on selected jewellery collections, and zero making charges on 24K certified investment coins.

Gold Hold & Grow (2%/month Benefit)

When you purchase or deposit physical gold (minimum 10 grams) with Shalom Gold Vaults for a 12-month tenure, Shalom Gold pays you a monthly benefit of 2% calculated on the deposited gold value directly into your verified bank account each month (equating to 24% annual equivalent).

Your gold is held in bank-grade, fully insured Tier-IV vaults with individual custody certificates and legal bailment documentation. It remains 100% your legal property at all times.

You can retrieve 100% of your initial physical gold deposit in 24K bullion bars, certified coins, or 22K jewellery from any Shalom Gold showroom with zero melting deductions, or opt to renew for another 12-month term.

Jewellery Purity & BIS Hallmarking

Every piece of jewellery at Shalom Gold carries the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) hallmark, including the BIS logo, purity fineness mark (e.g., 22K 916 or 18K 750), and a unique 6-digit alphanumeric Hallmarking Unique Identification (HUID) laser inscribed, verifiable on the government BIS Care app.

We provide a 100% lifetime buyback on gold weight at prevailing market spot rates without arbitrary melting deductions, ensuring complete liquidity for your family investments.
