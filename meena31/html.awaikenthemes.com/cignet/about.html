Our Founding Narrative

A Legacy Born in the Heart of Zaveri Bazaar

In 1985, Master Goldsmith Shri Ramdas Zaveri opened a quiet atelier in Mumbai’s historic Zaveri Bazaar with a singular conviction: that jewellery should never be treated merely as a commercial commodity, but as a sacred vessel of family legacy and enduring emotion.

Four decades later, Shalom Gold has evolved into one of India’s most revered fine jewellery and gold savings institutions, combining centuries-old royal crafting traditions with modern Karatmeter assay precision and bank-grade vault security.

The 4 Pillars of Shalom Gold Trust

Why generations of families across India make Shalom Gold their lifelong precious metals partner.

100% BIS Hallmarked

Every single jewel is engraved with the official government 6-digit HUID laser hallmark guaranteeing 916 (22K) or 750 (18K) gold purity.

Fair Open Pricing

Complete transparency with live gold market rates, clear weight receipts, and no concealed surcharges or melting deductions.

Zero Deduction Buyback

Lifetime guaranteed buyback at 100% of gold weight at prevailing market spot rates, without arbitrary melting deductions.
